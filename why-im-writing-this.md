A résumé tells you where someone has worked. It doesn't tell you how they think. This is the part that does.

I spend my days on the marketing side of multi-family — lifecycle email, content, listings, CRM, the unglamorous plumbing that quietly decides whether a community hits occupancy. The industry generates a steady stream of articles, reports, and hot takes about all of it. Most of them are worth reacting to; few of them get a real reaction in public.

So that's what this space is for. When something lands in my feed worth engaging with, I'll write up where I agree, where I don't, and what I'd actually do with it on the ground.

## What you'll find here

- **Responses to articles** — a link to the source, then my honest read.
- **Field notes** — patterns I'm seeing across communities and campaigns.
- **Things I changed my mind about** — because that's the useful part.

If you work in or around this space and want to compare notes, my inbox is open.
